## Proposed portfolio contract (our synthesis)

Use one primary Astral presence. Keep the entrance centred; after entry reserve a right-side area in the page grid. The right side should be designed as space for the guide, not an overlay added over full-width text. Put a small contextual label and an explicit Ask Astral action with it. A separate identical avatar in a floating chat button competes with that identity; consolidate the trigger in the eventual integrated design.

Motion has two levels. Continuous expression is low-amplitude breathing, twist and light circulation. A chapter change earns a single readable transformation: anticipation/compression → expansion/separation → new stable geometry. After settling, internal motion continues. Avoid endlessly repeating the full split/reassemble sequence during reading.

Suggested states: opening has an open ribbon; career shows a connected ribbon with distinct folds; project inspection separates three labelled layers; a response gathers the layers into a coherent form; unavailable retains a recognisable outline and clear written fallback. Project-specific graphics may remain, but must look like evidence/diagrams rather than a second assistant.

On mobile use one compact right-side guide in reserved header space, with a sheet on explicit tap. Larger transformations belong in dedicated full-width visual slots, not floating over paragraphs or buttons. Keep touch scrolling native. Pointer comet is secondary and fades; it is not the avatar and should not imply the assistant is following or profiling visitors.

## Best-practice grounding

[W3C Focus Not Obscured](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/focus-not-obscured-minimum) discusses sticky/overlay content hiding focused controls. Validate guide positioning against keyboard focus and zoom; reserve layout space where practical.

[W3C Pause, Stop, Hide](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/pause-stop-hide.html) covers applicable automatic movement over five seconds alongside content. Keep a working pause control for the user's continuous-motion preference. [Animation from Interactions](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/animation-from-interactions.html) is AAA and supports disabling non-essential interaction motion; distinguish it from the AA target rather than mislabelling it.

Next implementation should verify one clear primary avatar, labels/actions matching actual capabilities, no hidden focused controls, no content overlap at 320px/zoom, and equivalent manual/reduced-motion paths. Dribbble visual studies do not establish any of those outcomes by themselves.

This pass changes only the previously requested desktop anchor from left to right; the broader consolidated-avatar architecture remains a recommendation.
